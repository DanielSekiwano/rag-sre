            User Request: cajun style gumbo with an easy roux [/INST] Based on the recipes provided, to make a Cajun-style gumbo with an easy roux, you can follow these steps:

1. First, prepare the chicken by cutting it into 1-inch pieces and seasoning it with Cajun seasoning, salt, pepper, and thyme in a medium bowl. Set aside.
2. Heat oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Once hot, add the onion, bell pepper, carrots, celery, and garlic, cover and cook for 10 minutes or until the vegetables are crisp-tender. Stir once.
3. Add the seasoned chicken to the saucepan and cook for 3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Stir in flour to the chicken and vegetable mixture and cook for 1 minute, stirring frequently to make the roux.
5. Once the roux reaches a medium brown color, add chicken broth, tomatoes, and pepper sauce to the pot.
6. Bring the gumbo to a boil for 10 minutes and then lower heat, allowing it to simmer.
7. Serve the gumbo over rice.

This method uses an easier roux making process compared to the traditional method that involves waiting up to 2 hours. The roux in this recipe is made by cooking the flour and oil together with the vegetables and chicken, rather than waiting for the flour to brown on its own. This approach also allows for the vegetables to cook at the same time, making the process more efficient.</s>
